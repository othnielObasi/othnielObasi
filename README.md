## About Me

I build AI systems for **trusted autonomy** — where LLMs, agents, tools, data, and policies work together in real workflows with stronger **safety, evaluation, explainability, observability, and governance**.

My work focuses on the **execution layer of AI**: what happens after a model generates an output, when systems retrieve information, call tools, support decisions, trigger workflows, or operate under risk.

Across my projects, I combine **hands-on AI engineering**, **product thinking**, and **governance-first system design** — spanning LLM fine-tuning, model optimization, agentic workflows, memory-aware traces, audit logs, risk scoring, and deployed AI products.

### Model Optimization & Fine-Tuning

Worked on efficient model adaptation and deployment pipelines covering tokenizer training, QLoRA fine-tuning, distillation, quantization, TorchScript export, production inference, and model monitoring.

**Selected examples:**

- **Domain-Adapted Tokenizer & TinyLLaMA QLoRA** — trained a BPE tokenizer on a **500M-token biomedical corpus**, resized TinyLLaMA embeddings, and applied **4-bit QLoRA** with PEFT adapters for domain-specific QA, achieving a **12% perplexity drop** and **15% F1 lift** in evaluation.

- **Model Optimization & Inference Deployment Pipeline** — built an end-to-end pipeline for compact neural inference, covering training, distillation, quantization, TorchScript export, FastAPI serving, Supabase logging, Docker packaging, GCP Cloud Run deployment, and MLflow/W&B tracking. Retained **>98% task accuracy after quantization** while preserving a production-style serving and monitoring workflow.

**Focus:** *Tokenizer training, QLoRA, model optimization, quantization, distillation, deployment, and MLOps*


### Explainable AI & Decision Support

**Projects:** FraudSenseXAI · Legal Decision Support · Financial AI Systems  
**Key work:** SHAP, LIME, DiCE, ELI5, counterfactual explanations, feature-interaction graphs, model interpretation, and audit-focused decision support.  
**Focus:** *Explainable AI, fraud detection, legal AI, financial AI, decision support, auditability, and regulated workflows*
